How SwipeShrink: Photo Cleaner handles information when you use the app.

Effective August 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how SwipeShrink: Photo Cleaner (“SwipeShrink,” “the app,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you use the current Version 1 (V1) application. SwipeShrink is an iOS utility for reviewing, organizing, cleaning, and compressing photos and videos.

2. Photo Library Access

SwipeShrink needs access to Apple Photos (your Photo Library) to provide core functions such as finding duplicate or similar photos, organizing videos, calculating storage use, preparing deletion candidates, and creating compressed outputs.

Access is controlled by Apple’s permission system. Where iOS supports limited-library access, you can choose to make only selected photos and videos available. Some results may be incomplete, and some features may not work, if you deny access or provide limited access.

You can review or change SwipeShrink’s Photos permission through the appropriate privacy or app settings in iOS Settings. The exact labels and location may vary by iOS version. Reopen the app after changing permission if updated access is not immediately reflected.

3. Photos and Videos

The current V1 app accesses the media you permit through Apple’s Photos framework and processes that media locally on your device. Accessing your library through PhotoKit does not mean the developer receives your photos or videos.

The developer does not operate a server to which the current V1 app uploads your photos or videos. SwipeShrink also does not require you to create a SwipeShrink account.

4. On-Device Analysis

To provide its features, SwipeShrink may examine file metadata and visual or content characteristics needed for functions including:

  • identifying possible duplicate photos;
  • grouping visually similar photos;
  • categorizing and organizing media;
  • calculating file sizes and local storage use;
  • organizing videos; and
  • evaluating and producing compression results.

This analysis takes place on your device in the current V1 implementation. Duplicate and similarity results are review aids, not guarantees that media is identical or safe to delete.

5. Photo Deletion

Photo or video deletion is initiated only by you. Swipe Delete may help you review or stage deletion candidates, but the final operation follows Apple’s Photos/PhotoKit authorization mechanisms and may require system confirmation. SwipeShrink cannot bypass Apple’s Photos permission or deletion confirmation.

Review before deleting. Carefully inspect every selected photo and video, and retain backups of important media where appropriate, before confirming deletion.

The developer does not silently initiate deletion. Any recovery options, including Apple Photos’ Recently Deleted feature, depend on the user’s device and system state and are not guaranteed.

6. Photo Compression

Compression operates on media you select after you deliberately choose a compression action. The current workflow creates a new compressed JPEG output. You choose whether to save that output back to Photos, and saving occurs through PhotoKit after your explicit action or confirmation.

SwipeShrink does not automatically replace or delete the original photo when it creates or saves a compressed copy. You should compare the output and retain the original until you are satisfied with the result.

7. Information the Developer Receives

Under the current V1 implementation, SwipeShrink does not require an account and does not include developer-operated photo or video uploads, analytics, advertising, or tracking systems. Accordingly, the app is not designed to transmit your Photo Library content or on-device analysis results to the developer.

If you contact support by email, the developer receives the information you intentionally include in that message, such as your email address, device details, and description of the issue. Please do not send private photos or videos unless you intentionally choose to do so and believe it is necessary.

This description is limited to the current V1 behavior. Future features that change data handling will require this policy to be reviewed and updated before those features are released.

8. Data Selling, Advertising, and Tracking

Current V1 does not sell user photo or video content, use that content for third-party advertising, or track users across other companies’ apps or websites. The app does not currently contain advertising or analytics systems.

9. Third-Party Platform Services

SwipeShrink relies on Apple iOS and the Photos/PhotoKit framework for device permissions and Photo Library operations. Your use of Apple services is also governed by Apple’s applicable terms and policies. Apple is not the developer of SwipeShrink, and reference to Apple services does not imply Apple’s endorsement of the app.

10. Children’s Privacy

SwipeShrink is a general-purpose photo utility and is not designed to knowingly solicit personal information from children. The current V1 app does not include account registration or age-gating features. If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support request, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

11. Security

On-device processing and Apple’s permission controls help limit exposure of your media. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the app’s current design, but no device, software, or method of handling information can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and Apple account.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in SwipeShrink, legal requirements, or data-handling practices. An updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes should be reviewed before the related app behavior is released.
